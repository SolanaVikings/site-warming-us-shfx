Maintenance & Power Flush

Power Flushing, Sludge Removal & Heating Diagnostics

Cold radiators, sludge in the system, noisy boilers and weak heat — diagnosed before anything gets replaced. Often what looks like a broken boiler is a blocked one.

What we cover

  • Full system power flush
  • Sludge removal and magnetic filter fitting
  • Radiator balancing across the property
  • Pump, valve and motorised valve replacement
  • Pressure top-ups and expansion vessel checks
  • Cold-spot diagnosis on individual radiators
  • System health checks before a boiler swap

Why it matters

A flushed system runs quieter, heats faster and protects the boiler warranty on a new install. Most "broken" boilers are blocked rather than dead — diagnosis first saves the cost of a replacement that wasn't needed.

Typical pricing

A full power flush in most South Wales homes runs £350–£550, fixed after a quick check on system size and condition.

Coverage

Cardiff, Penarth, Barry, Caerphilly, Bridgend, Newport and the Valleys.

Before you ask

Quick Answers.
No Runaround.

How quickly can you come out?

For emergencies, we prioritise Cardiff and South Wales call outs. Booked work is usually same-week, often sooner.

Do you give fixed quotes before starting?

Yes. You get a clear quote upfront. If the scope changes, we agree it with you before carrying on.

What if something goes wrong after you've left?

Workmanship is covered for 12 months on installations. Manufacturer warranties apply to new parts and boilers.

Do you cover my area?

We cover Cardiff, Penarth, Barry, Caerphilly, Bridgend, Newport, the Valleys and wider South Wales.

What does a power flush usually cost?

In most South Wales homes a full system power flush runs £350–£550 depending on the number of radiators, system age and how much sludge has built up. We give you a fixed price after the initial check.

Do you fit smart thermostats like Hive or Nest?

Yes. We install Hive, Nest, Tado and Honeywell controls — either as a stand-alone upgrade or as part of a boiler swap. Wiring and Wi-Fi pairing are included.

How long does a boiler installation take?

A like-for-like boiler swap is usually a one-day job. A full heating upgrade — new boiler, controls, system flush and rebalancing — typically runs 1 to 3 days, scheduled in advance so you're not without heat overnight.

Do you handle CP12 certificates for landlords across Cardiff?

Yes. We do CP12 gas safety certificates for landlords across Cardiff, Penarth, Barry, Caerphilly, Bridgend, Newport and the Valleys. The certificate is emailed and posted the same day, and we keep a record on file for re-issue.

Which boiler brands do you install?

We install Vaillant, Worcester-Bosch, Baxi, Glow-worm, Ideal and Viessmann. We can match a brand you already have, or recommend the right one for the property and household — and the manufacturer warranty stands when we install it.

Why does my radiator stay cold at the top?

A cold top usually means trapped air — bleeding the radiator fixes it. A cold bottom means sludge sitting in the radiator and the system needs flushing. Either way we'll diagnose before suggesting a replacement.
